The whole game, playable in the browser: the title, the menu, every year's map of the war, and the battles, the forts and the trains, to the war's end. Every routine is ported from the game's own 6502 code and was run beside it until the two agreed.

Loading the game's data…
  • ← → ↑ ↓ and Right Ctrl: joystick 2, the Union's (and one player's)
  • Numpad 8 2 4 6 and 0: joystick 1, the Confederacy's when two play
  • Menu and map: the cursor; fire clicks. With the keyboard chosen: P L Z X and Space
  • Battle: Esc (RUN/STOP) or F5 chooses the unit; E or Page Up (the up-arrow) falls back
  • Battle, fort and train: H pauses, any key goes on
  • Map: Alt (the Commodore key) stops a war the computer plays on both sides

The game's own screen, drawn from the port's memory each frame as the video chip would draw it, at the C64's own pace. The game came on four tape sides and loaded each part as it was needed: here a load is instant, but the game's prompts to turn the tape over still wait for fire.
