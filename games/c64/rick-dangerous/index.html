Commodore 64 · 1989 · Firebird

Rick Dangerous

Core Design's platform game of traps, sticks and dynamite. Its Hall of Fame keeps a cheat behind the name BBOOIINNGG, refuses eleven rude words, and at power-on can slip NOBBY or JEZEBEL into the table by itself.

01 · In play

Six of everything

Rick starts each level with six bullets and six sticks of dynamite, and the game with six lives. The panel above the cave draws them as rows of bullets, red sticks and hats, beside a six-digit score. The bullets and the dynamite go back to six after every life lost, as well as at the start of each level. The lives do not.

The first room, a moment after the start. The counters are at $2D32 (bullets), $2D33 (dynamite), and $2D34 (lives).

02 · Secret

Secret: the name BBOOIINNGG turns on a cheat

A score good enough for the Hall of Fame lets the player spell a name of up to ten letters with the joystick, pressing fire for each. The routine at $2146 then compares the name, letter by letter, with two words kept at $21B8: FLUFOMATIC and BBOOIINNGG. BBOOIINNGG sets a flag at $53, and from then on the main loop listens for two keys.

KeyWith the flag set
2bullets, dynamite, and lives back to six
C=straight to the next level's story screen

Both keys sit on keyboard row 7, the only row the game ever selects, and the same lines carry the joystick in port 1. With the flag set, pushing a stick in port 1 to the right refills the counters as well. FLUFOMATIC sets a second flag, at $37, whose effect has not been found.

03 · The Hall of Fame

Eleven words the Hall of Fame refuses

As soon as a name is finished, and before the cheat words are compared, the routine at $2103 looks through it for any of eleven four-letter words stored at $2181. They run from the obvious ones to BOLL, ARSE, and BROS. A name that contains one goes into the table as SNIGGER.

The Hall of Fame the game starts on. The names are stored column by column at $2318: the first letters of all eight names, then the second letters, and so on.

04 · Secret

Secret: the Hall of Fame can change at power-on

Once, as the game starts, the routine at $226A reads the raster line the video chip is drawing and uses it as a random number. When the line's top bit is set, the routine copies NOBBY or JEZEBEL, both kept at $2368, over one of the names in the table. Which name goes, and which replaces it, depends on where the beam was at that instant, so two boots of the same tape can open on different Halls of Fame.

05 · Controls

The quit key is ←

The manual says the up-arrow key quits to the start. In the game, ↑ does nothing during play. The key the main loop tests, at $0A95, is ←, the arrow at the top left of the keyboard: pressing it ends the game with GAME OVER.

06 · The story

The ending is in memory from the first room

The tape loads once. The text for all five story screens, one before each level and one for the ending, sits together at $AD00 while the first level is played.
